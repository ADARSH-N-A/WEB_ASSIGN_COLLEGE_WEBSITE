About the Department

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering at FISAT focuses on quality education in computing. It encourages research, innovation and technical skills to prepare students for industry and society.

Vision

To achieve excellence in computer science education, research and professional development.

Mission

To provide quality education, promote innovation and develop technically skilled and socially responsible professionals.

Academic Programmes

  • B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (4 years)
  • M.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (2 years)
  • Ph.D. in Computer Science (Research)

Core Subjects Offered

  1. Data Structures and Algorithms
  2. Database Management Systems
  3. Operating Systems
  4. Computer Networks
  5. Web Programming
  6. Machine Learning
